Research interests

Lifelong Learning, Bayesian Inference, Hyperspectral Image Analysis, and Uncertainty Quantification and Calibration.

01

Bayesian Optimization

Bayesian approaches to efficient search and decision-making under uncertainty.

02

Hyperspectral Image Analysis

Static and temporal hyperspectral analysis, foundation model pretraining, classification, land cover segmentation, and change detection.

03

Uncertainty Quantification and Calibration of LLMs

Probabilistic adaptation of language models, with a focus on predictive uncertainty, calibration, and reliable generalization.

04

Lifelong / Continual Learning

Learning new tasks while preserving prior knowledge through variational inference, probabilistic experts, and adaptive model representations.
